I design and build products, which these days means live video software sitting on top of a lot of fairly unforgiving infrastructure. For a good few years I also wrote a lot on this site: long series about whatever I happened to be learning at the time, write-ups of things I'd built, and the occasional four thousand words on something I thought was rubbish.

Since 2015 I've been at id3as, who spent years building bespoke live video systems for broadcasters and streaming companies and have since turned all of that into a product company, Norsk. Underneath it's Erlang, Purescript, Rust, C and CUDA, with TypeScript on top for the people who have to talk to it.

My job there is mostly working out what we should build, with customers in the room where possible, and then going and building it. The biggest piece of that so far is Norsk Studio. I built the first version over a weekend with the goal of "Windows Forms for media workflows", and it has since become the thing the rest of the Norsk product range is built on (Playout, Remote Commentary and friends).

Before that I was a Technical Expert at some of the largest strategic consultancies in the world, then a freelancer, a regular contributor to RavenDB, and for most of 2013 a wandering developer working in exchange for a roof over my head. I used to speak at conferences quite a lot before the start-up life took me.

All of the old writing is still here. Writing has the series and everything else by year, and there are projects and talks too.
